Born from the pro peloton’s growing preference for lace precision, the M Pro Lace translates the pursuit of the perfect fit to every rider delivering serious effort on the road. The two-tone lacing system delivers even pressure distribution across the foot, creating a locked-in connection that sustains comfort and efficiency from sharp accelerations to all-day efforts.

Sharing its foundation with the acclaimed M3 Pro, every detail is engineered for maximal foot stability, streamlined aerodynamics and effective power transfer. A sculpted heel cup delivers a zero-slip hold, the vibration-absorbing insole cushions against road fatigue, and a breathable mesh-TPU construction offers ventilation without compromising structure. A laminated toe border resists wear — keeping the M Pro Lace pristine and ready to deliver as the ultimate road racing shoe.

FEATURES

Failproof Lace Closure

Two-tone laces deliver even pressure distribution across the entire foot, with an elegant aesthetic and fail-safe reliability.

Race-Proven Carbon Sole

Full-carbon sole delivers maximum power transfer, built on the same Tour-validated platform as the M3 Pro.

Aerated Mesh TPU Upper

Triple-layer technical mesh with crisscrossed TPU structure for secure, lightweight ventilation and reduced drag at speed.

TPU Toe Border

Protective TPU panels around the toe box resist scuffs and scrapes, maintaining pristine race-day aesthetics all season.

Sculpted Heel Cup

Moulded heel retention with refined last and padded collar ensures zero slip during sprints and aggressive cornering.
